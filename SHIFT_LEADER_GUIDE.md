# BOH Leaders app: Shift Leader guide

**Open:** https://roudic.github.io/boh-leader-pathway/ → **Sign in with Google** (the account Joshua added).
Tip: on your phone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Every shift
**End-of-shift summary** (📝 tab) before you leave: add **Wins, Issues, People** (who stood out, who needs coaching), and **Follow-ups**. Tap **Send to Joshua**. It goes to his inbox in the app.

Area checklists and Pathways training are in their own apps, not this one.

## P5 Evaluation (⭐ tab), about 5 minutes
1. Pick the Captain or Assistant Captain, area, AM/PM.
2. Tap **1–4** for each of the 20 behaviors. Score only what you **saw this shift**.
   1 = Not seen · 2 = Sometimes · 3 = Consistent · 4 = Leads others in it
3. Any **1 or 2** means you **must** write an example in that P's notes box (what, when, where).
4. The total updates live. **72/80 (90%) = Pass.** Any P under 12/16 gets marked **Coaching focus**, even when they pass.
5. Fill in strengths, next step, and follow-up date. Check the **MOD** box, hand them the phone to check theirs, then **Submit**.

## Coaching notes
Open anyone's profile (Home → their name) → **Add coaching note**. Leave "Let them see this note" **off** unless you want them to read it.

## Rules of thumb
- Score only what you saw.
- Stuck or something looks wrong? Tell Joshua. Don't guess.
